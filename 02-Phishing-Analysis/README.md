## Simulated Email Investigation

### Scenario
An employee receives an email claiming to be from the company's IT security team. The message warns that the employee's account will be disabled unless they verify their login information within 30 minutes.

### Evidence Reviewed
- **Sender:** support@company-security-alert.example
- **Subject:** URGENT: Your Account Will Be Disabled
- **Link:** https://company-login-verify.example
- **Requested Action:** Verify login information within 30 minutes.

**Note:** This investigation uses fictional training data. No live emails, email headers, or websites were analyzed.

### Identified Phishing Indicators

**1. Unverified Sender Address**

The sender claims to represent the company's IT department, but the email domain has not been verified as an authorized company domain.

**2. Urgency and Fear Tactics**

The email threatens account suspension within 30 minutes. This creates pressure on the recipient to act quickly without verifying the message.

**3. Suspicious Verification Link**

The message directs the recipient to an unverified website to enter login information. This could be an attempt to steal account credentials.

### Risk Assessment

**Classification:** Suspected Phishing Attempt

**Potential Security Risks:**
- Credential theft
- Unauthorized account access
- Exposure of sensitive company information
- Account compromise

### Recommended Security Response

1. Do not click the suspicious link.
2. Do not enter usernames or passwords.
3. Verify account status through the company's official website.
4. Contact the IT security department using an approved communication channel.
5. Report the suspicious email to the security team.
6. Preserve the email for further investigation according to company procedures.

### Investigation Conclusion

After reviewing the simulated email, I identified three primary phishing indicators: an unverified sender address, urgency and fear-based language, and a suspicious verification link.

Based on these findings, I classified the message as a suspected phishing attempt.

I recommended that the recipient avoid interacting with the link, independently verify the account notification, and report the email to the appropriate security team.

This investigation demonstrates my ability to recognize common phishing techniques, assess potential security risks, and recommend safe response procedures.
